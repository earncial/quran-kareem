Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Qur'an Kareem app.

Is the Qur'an text authentic?
Yes. The Arabic text displayed in Qur'an Kareem is sourced directly from the Tanzil Project. It is 100 percent original, completely untouched, and unmodified.
Is Qur'an Kareem completely free?
Yes, Qur'an Kareem is a 100 percent free application with no hidden charges, subscriptions, or intrusive ads. We believe everyone should have free access to the Qur'an.
Can Imams use this for real time search?
Absolutely. The built in offline search engine is highly optimized. Scholars and Imams can search the entire Qur'an and find exactly what they need within seconds, even without internet.
How does the memorization mode work?
Students can use the hide and reveal feature. This allows you to hide the Ayah text to test your memory, and reveal it to check if you are correct.
Who developed and published Qur'an Kareem?
Qur'an Kareem is developed and published by Earncial Global. You can follow our official updates on TikTok at @quran.ng.
